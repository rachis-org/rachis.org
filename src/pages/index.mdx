<Section id="why-rachis" eyebrow="What your methods gain">
  <Fragment slot="title">

## Give your methods a shared framework.

  </Fragment>
  <div className="rachis-grid grid-four">
    <Card>
      <Fragment slot="title">

### Decentralized data provenance

      </Fragment>

Rachis automatically records the inputs, parameters, software versions, and upstream results behind an analysis. Provenance replay can turn the recorded steps into code to replicate the results or provide a starting point for a new analysis.
    </Card>
    <Card>
      <Fragment slot="title">

### Validation and type checking

      </Fragment>

Describe the semantic data your method accepts and produces, along with its parameters. Rachis uses these definitions to check inputs and guide how methods can be composed together.
    </Card>
    <Card>
      <Fragment slot="title">

### Multiple user interfaces

      </Fragment>

Annotate your method as a plugin once and rachis will make it available through command-line, Python, and graphical interfaces. Users can choose the interface that fits their work, from an exploratory notebook, an HPC workflow, or even a shareable visualization URL.
    </Card>
    <Card>
      <Fragment slot="title">

### Methods that work together

      </Fragment>

A shared language of types and methods means that work can be reused and applied in many different contexts. Methods become generalizable building blocks for large scale reproducible analyses and higher-level tooling.
    </Card>

  </div>
</Section>

### Built to stand the test of time.

Artifacts (`.qza`) and visualizations (`.qzv`) are standard ZIP archives. Their data, metadata, and provenance can be inspected without a centralized service.

[Look inside a result](https://develop.qiime2.org/en/stable/framework/explanations/archives.html)

### The history stays with the result.

A collaborator can inspect the recorded analysis and use provenance replay to re-generate code for the steps that produced it. The `provenance/artifacts/` directory preserves the history of upstream results.

[Learn about provenance replay](https://forum.qiime2.org/t/provenance-replay-beta-release-and-tutorial/23279)

### Verify what you share.

Optional cryptographic signatures let collaborators verify a result's signer and integrity when sharing it.

[Sign and verify results](https://use.qiime2.org/en/stable/how-to-guides/sign-and-verify-artifacts/)
